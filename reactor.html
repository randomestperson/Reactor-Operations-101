Reactor Operations 101

Crash course on operating a 1500 MW RBMK Reactor

Introduction

This page is mainly a page on why I made this project, my sources and general information about the context.

I made this project to provide a comprehensive guide on operating a 1500 MW RBMK Reactor, to be specfic the I-CWNR-1000 reactor model. The I-CWNR-1000 is a variation of the RBMK reactor that was implemented into a factory game I play alot called "Industrialist". I would say that the reactor itself, even though being heavily simplified is still very realistic. The game consists of the player constructing large factiores from simple pipes, machines and refineries. It has a steep learning curve and acctually requires alot of math to properly create production ratios. My idea for this project was somewhat of my own take on how to start, operate and maintain the in-game reactor. For context, the reactor is one of the last items you unlock in the game. So most people don't even know what it looks like. The official wiki page for the game already has a guide but I would say it is missing alot of elements that an average player wouldn't know (for instance, how to flod the reactor hall. It took me 5 minutes to look for that switch). So I decided I would create a more detailed guide.
