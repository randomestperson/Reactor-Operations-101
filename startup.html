Startup Procedure

Step-by-step guide to starting up the RBMK Reactor

The startup procedure is the most crucial procedure and where the most things can go wrong if not followed correctly.

Startup Procedure

  1. Check for any faulty systems or malfunctions.
  2. Turn on all the coolant pumps and set the Flowrate slider to 100%.
  3. Select all the heat exchangers.
  4. Set the Steam and Water valve at the heat exchanger panel to 100% and click Set Value
  5. Turn off the OCCS (Offline Core Cooling Systems).
  6. Set the startup heater to On
  7. Set Heaters and Fine Control Heater to 100%
  8. Set the startup heater to Off once water temperature surpasses 160°C
  9. Begin slowly removing the control rods using the insertion slider.
  10. Watch the thermal generation as it rises. As it increases, Xenon and Pressurizer Pressure should increase. You may notice the reactor core shifting colours. If the core changes to black, IMMEDIATELY insert the control rods.
  11. Once thermal generation has reached around 4GMW/s, begin to stabilize the reactor. Then, move onto the turbine run-up section.

Turbine Run-Up

Once the reactor is stable, you can begin to run up the turbines.

  1. Once the reactor is stable, set one of the turbine's bypass valve to 40% and wait for the turbine to reach 3500 RPM.
  2. When nearing 3500 RPM, Begin increasing the bypass valve so that the turbine speed change becomes +5 RPM.
  3. Once you see that RPM is at 3590-3610 with an RPM change of ±5, click the Turbine Synchronization Button
  4. From here, slowly increase Generator Engagement and lower the Bypass Valve until you get to 100% Engagement and 0% Bypass Valve (do not let RPM Change to get out of the ±5 range or else the Turbines will desync and you'll have to adjust the Bypass Valve to get it back down to 3590-3610 RPM).
  5. Repeat steps for the next 3 Turbines until you have finished syncing the Turbines.

Grid Management

Once all turbines are synchronized, connect them to the grid.

  1. Contact your grid operator to request connection.
  2. Ensure turbine output does not exceed 200MW/s otherwise they will trip.

Proceed to operation procedure once started
