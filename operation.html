Standard Operating Procedures

These procedures outline the steps for the safe and efficient operation of the reactor. The procedures are relatively simple.

  1. Ensure reactor core does NOT turn black, ever. If it does, insert control rods until issue is resolved.
  2. Ensure thermal generation does not exceed safe limits (5GW/s). In the event that it does, initiate the SCRAM.
  3. Monitor for any malfunctions or anomalies in the reactor systems.

If you follow all these procedures, you should be able to operate the reactor safely and efficiently. Happy operating!
