Crash course on operating a 1500 MW RBMK Reactor

Pre-Startup Procedure

The pre-startup procedure is a very crucial part of operating the reactor. It ensures that when the startup is complete, there will be no additional tasks required such as enabling cooling valves or setting temperatures for specfic systems. This procedure allows the operator to run the reactor with less stress and more comfort.

Learn more

Startup Procedure

The startup procedure is the most important and most dangerous procedure in the operation of a nuclear reactor. It is very important that every operator properly pays attention to the startup guide otherwise it could cost them their life. This procedure is what gets the reactor running and providing power to the electrical grid. Many things can go wrong here, so pay close attention to instructions.
